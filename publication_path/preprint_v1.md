# Geometry of fine-tuning in a small audio-encoder family

**Status: v1 working draft for TMLR.** Revised from v0 (chat draft 2026-04-28) following Opus 4.7 extended-thinking peer review. v1 incorporates: (a) softened semantic vocabulary throughout (concern 1); (b) explicit framing of random-init as a "preserves input acoustics" control rather than a "no structure" control (concern 2); (c) the SSL-fine-tune-introduces-new-data-domain confound flagged in §1 and §8 (concern 3); (d) reframe of §3 from "pooling distorts geometry" to "frame-level and pooled views diverge heterogeneously" (concern 5); (e) reframe of §4.8 from "factored hierarchy" to "drift toward random-orthogonality floor that random-init does not exhibit" (concern 6); (f) new §4.11 on linear-probe corroboration of §4.7 (concern 1 / 7); (g) softened §5.2 — L12 retains Order-discriminative information in low-eigenvalue directions, so the "thrown out everything except bio" reading is too strong.

Manifest-resampling results (concern 4) are folded into §4.8 and the Limitations section: the headline §4.8 number for `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` is across-resample-stable (mean 0.048 ± 0.020 over 5 stratified-resample seeds at 75% retention, smaller spread than the within-manifest bootstrap CI). The reviewer's prediction that across-manifest spread would be "several × the within-manifest CI" is refuted for the headline model, though it holds for the intermediate-cos models which are not headline findings.

## Abstract

We probe four 13-layer audio transformers from the Earth Species Project's ESP-AVES2 EAT family — one bio-pretrained checkpoint, one general-audio checkpoint, and the SSL fine-tunes of each — against two frozen subsamples of NatureLM-audio-training: a 600-clip source-stratified manifest (100 × 7 source datasets, used for the bio/non-bio and pooling findings) and an 800-clip per-Order manifest (4 Aves Orders × 100 + 200 Mammalia + 200 non-bio, used for the Class / Order / species findings). Activations from all 13 layers of each model are anchored against a fifth control: the same EAT architecture with completely re-initialized weights. We find that the four trained models are not interchangeable. One checkpoint — the bio-pretrained model with SSL fine-tuning on the bio + non-bio union — exhibits geometric properties at multiple scales that none of its three trained siblings or the random-init control exhibit: it (i) separates bio from non-bio at the subspace level mid-network, (ii) produces the strongest linearly-recoverable Class direction in the family (Aves vs Mammalia at layer 7, probe accuracy 0.96 and subspace cos 0.40), (iii) preserves the most within-Aves species structure of any trained model, and (iv) reaches the random-orthogonality floor for the cosine between Class and within-Aves Order centroid-displacement directions at the output layer, while the random-init control does not. We do not claim this constitutes a *factored* hierarchical representation — that claim requires causal evidence we do not have — but the four geometric properties consistently single out the same checkpoint. Beyond the headline, we report two methodological notes: frame-level and mean-pooled views of the geometry diverge heterogeneously across (model, layer); and the relationship between the linear envelope (effective rank) and the local manifold (MLE-ID) decouples during training, with the eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio rising from ≈1 in random init to 17–43 in trained models while the manifold-dim estimate itself stays in 7–14. We make no claim about audio encoders generally; the population is a single architecture's four checkpoints plus its random-init control.

## 1. Introduction and scope

**What this paper is.** A within-family geometric audit of one published audio-encoder family. The pretraining-data axis (bio-only vs bio+non-bio) and the SSL-fine-tune axis (with/without) cross to give 4 cells; we add architecture-only random-init as a fifth. We hold the manifest, extraction protocol, and metric implementations fixed across all five, run frame-level analyses on 30,000 frames per (model, layer), and bootstrap each headline number.

**What this paper is not.** A claim about audio encoders in general, a claim about pooling in general, or a claim that we have found semantic features. We take centroids and subspaces seriously as descriptions of where mass sits in 768-dim space; we do not conflate "Aves and Mammalia centroids are far apart along a stable direction" with "the model has learned a bird concept."

**Why the EAT family.** Four checkpoints sharing architecture, training corpus structure, and tokenization, varying only in two binary training-axis choices, is rare. The minimal-pairs design lets us attribute geometric differences to ingredient combinations rather than to architectural confounds.

**Two unavoidable confounds the n=4 design cannot disentangle.** (a) With no replicates per cell, "the combination of bio-pretrain × SSL-fine-tune produces this geometry" is observationally indistinguishable from "the specific training run that produced `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` happened to land in a favorable initialization / data-order / learning-rate-schedule trajectory." (b) The SSL fine-tune is on the bio+non-bio union for both SSL variants, which means `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` is exposed to a *new* acoustic domain (non-bio) at fine-tune time on top of bio pretrain, while `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is not. The "SSL fine-tune" axis is therefore confounded with "exposure to a new acoustic domain at fine-tune time," and that second factor — not the SSL objective itself — is plausibly the active ingredient in any geometry difference between `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` and `eat_bio`. We name this confound here so readers can apply the appropriate skepticism throughout.

**The headline finding in one sentence.** Of the four trained checkpoints, only `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` (bio pretrain + SSL fine-tune) simultaneously exhibits a bio-vs-non-bio direction, the highest linearly-recoverable Class structure, within-Aves species structure, and a Class/Order centroid-cosine at the random-orthogonality floor at the output layer; the combination of ingredients matters relative to either ingredient alone, with the caveat above.

## 2. Setup and the random-init anchor

We use the standard EAT extraction (`model.pos_drop` + `model.blocks.0..11`, hidden dim 768, 513 patch tokens including a CLS-like index 0) on each of two frozen manifests. Frame-level subsampling draws 50 frames per clip from the valid-token range with seed 42; MLE-ID and TwoNN further subsample to 10,000 frames.

**Two manifests, used by different sections.** The findings in this paper come from two pre-registered subsamples of NatureLM-audio-training:

- **Source manifest** — `naturelm_by_source_100each_20260418T171459Z`, **600 clips = 100 × 7 source datasets** (Xeno-canto, iNaturalist, Animal Sound Archive, Watkins as bio; WavCaps, NatureLM-music, NSynth as non-bio). Used by §3 (frame-vs-pooled), §4 (bio-vs-non-bio direction), §4.5 (bio-axis mixing), §4.6 (per-source structure), §5 / §5.1 / §5.2 / §5.3 / §5.4 (late-layer collapse), §8 (eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio).
- **Per-Order manifest** — `naturelm_by_order_p100_m200_n200_20260427T222756Z`, **800 clips = 4 Aves Orders × 100 + 200 Mammalia + 200 non-bio**. Constructed because the source manifest has only 11 non-Passeriformes Aves clips, which is too few to test within-Aves Order structure at any reasonable statistical strength. Used by §4.7 (Aves vs Mammalia), §4.8 (Veitch hierarchy test), §4.9 (species barycenters), §4.11 (linear-probe corroboration), and the manifest-resampling sensitivity in §10 / Limitations.

The two manifests share the same extraction pipeline and the same five models but cover different population structures; numbers from one cannot be directly compared to numbers from the other without restating which manifest was used. We name the source manifest by default in headline numbers and explicitly tag per-Order numbers as such throughout. The frame-level row count per (model, layer) is 30,000 on the source manifest (600 × 50) and 40,000 on the per-Order manifest (800 × 50).

The random-init baseline (`random_init_eat_seed42`) is the same EAT architecture instantiated and then completely reinitialized — `init_weights` plus per-module `reset_parameters`, with a `normal(0, 0.02)` fallback for the 2/150 parameters those paths miss. It receives the same audio through the same hooks. Init variability is checked at seeds 7 and 13 (eff_rank spread ≤ 1.3 across all layers vs trained-vs-random gaps of 200–350); we keep seed 42 as the anchor and treat the seed-spread as negligible relative to every claim's effect size.

**What random-init does and does not control for.** Random-init *passes audio through random transformer weights*; it does not output noise. The activations preserve substantial input-acoustic structure — random projections approximately preserve Euclidean distances, and patch-token attention with random weights still mixes acoustically-similar frames. We will see in §4.11 that random-init produces a Class probe accuracy of 0.85 (Aves vs Mammalia, majority baseline 0.67) — random-init "knows" Aves from Mammalia at 85% from acoustic input statistics alone, before any training. Random-init is therefore a "preserves input acoustic structure" control, not a "no structure" control. Where trained models differ from random-init we cannot in general distinguish "trained models added structure" from "trained models destroyed input structure"; both are happening. We flag specific cases in §4.9 (where random-init preserves *more* per-species detail than trained models, because trained models compress fine acoustic differences in service of coarser invariances) and in §5/§6 (where the comparison between random-init and `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` reads more meaningful than it is, because the two arrive at similar effective rank by opposite routes).

We use four geometric primitives: **effective rank** = `exp(-Σ p_i log p_i)` over normalized covariance eigenvalues; **participation ratio** = `(Σλ)² / Σλ²`; **intrinsic dimension** via TwoNN(k=2) and MLE-ID(k=20); **subspace overlap** = `mean(cos(principal_angles))` between top-10 PCA bases. All headlines are accompanied by B=30 to B=50 bootstrap CIs on the manifest.

## 3. Frame-level and mean-pooled views diverge heterogeneously across (model, layer)

Mean-pooled effective rank reads ≈ 3 at layer 0 of all four trained models. Frame-level effective rank at L0 reads 11/26/44/17 across the four. At later layers the divergence runs the other way: pooled TwoNN reads 9–11 across most (model, layer), while frame-level TwoNN drops to 3–5. The two views of the same activations diverge by a factor that depends on the (model, layer) cell.

We do not frame this as "pooling distorts geometry" — frame-level is not ground truth, and either view can be the right one depending on the downstream object of analysis. If a user of these embeddings will pool them for downstream classification, the *pooled* geometry is what matters for that user. What we do claim is that the divergence is heterogeneous: a finding about one view does not transfer cleanly to the other. The "L0 effective rank ≈ 3 across all four models" finding from an earlier version of this work is a case study — it survived because pooling collapsed heterogeneous L0 subspaces onto similar low-dim numbers, and the four L0 subspaces are visibly different at frame level. Every subsequent section uses frame-level activations because the geometric primitives in §2 (effective rank, MLE-ID, principal-angle subspace cosines) are defined on the unaggregated frame distribution; we do not claim frame-level geometry is privileged in general.

## 4. The bio direction lives in only one model and exhibits a non-linear input/representation map

**§4 Bio-vs-non-bio subspace cosine.** Top-10 subspace cosine between bio-pooled and non-bio-pooled centroids' PCA bases drops to **0.569 [0.556, 0.605]** at L9 of `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`. The other three trained models bottom out in [0.67, 0.81] in the same layer range; the random-init baseline reads 0.90 [0.89, 0.92]. The gap between `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` and the random-init anchor is 30× the within-manifest CI width.

**§4.5 The bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis exhibits an asymmetric input/representation map.** Mixing 25% non-bio audio into a bio recording at the waveform level drags the L9 representation 78% of the way to pure non-bio along the centroid axis. This is a single mix ratio, not a sweep, so it is consistent with several functional forms — a saturating non-linearity, a piecewise-linear response with a low-slope regime in 0–5%, or even a roughly-linear response if the bio cluster's scale is small relative to the 25%-mix step. The asymmetry — a small acoustic perturbation producing a disproportionately large representational move — is *specific to* `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`. The other three trained models have near-zero or partial bio-axis range, so the question of functional form is not even meaningful on them. We do not claim the response is threshold-like; we claim only that the wide bio-axis is unlocked by the *combination* of bio pretrain and SSL fine-tuning, and that within that wide axis the input-to-representation map is asymmetric. A mixing-ratio sweep distinguishing linear from saturating from threshold is queued.

**§4.6 "Bio" is not a single coherent direction in any of the trained models.** Per-source pairwise frame-level distances reveal that within-bio source-pairs do not collapse onto a single point; Watkins (marine mammals) is geometrically more distant from Xeno-canto than several non-bio sources are from each other. The bio-vs-non-bio direction in §4 should be read as "the model separates the four bio source distributions from the three non-bio source distributions on average," not "the model has learned a bio-detector feature."

## 5. Class and within-Aves Order structure across the family

**§4.7 Aves vs Mammalia subspace cosine is lowest in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`.** Per-Class top-10 subspace cosine drops to **0.403 [0.386, 0.423]** at L7 of `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` — beating every other learned direction in this paper. The other trained models read 0.57–0.65 in the same layer range; random-init reads 0.91. The gap to random-init is 28× the within-manifest CI. We add a non-geometric corroboration of this finding in §4.11.

**§4.8 At L12, the cosine between the Aves-Mammalia direction and the Passeriformes-Aves direction reaches the 768-d random-orthogonality floor in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`, which the random-init control does not.** Following Veitch et al.'s hierarchical-feature framing, we test |cos(Aves−Mammalia direction, Order−Aves direction)| at every layer. Only `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches near-zero values: L9 cos = 0.14, **L12 cos = 0.033 [0.0036, 0.110]**. None of the other trained models drop below 0.30. Random-init stays at [0.88, 0.98].

**The expected |cos| of two independently chosen unit vectors in 768-d is √(2/(πd)) ≈ 0.029.** The headline 0.033 is statistically indistinguishable from this random-orthogonality floor, and the lower CI bound 0.0036 is below it. The reported number is therefore *consistent* with two arbitrary centroid-difference directions in 768-d, and we cannot read it as evidence that the model has *factored* Class and Order into orthogonal feature subspaces. The contrast that the test does establish is asymmetric: random-init reads 0.88–0.98, far from the random-orthogonality floor — meaning the random-init network has substantial *alignment* between within-Aves and between-Class displacements, and trained models drift away from that alignment. `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches the random-orthogonality floor most cleanly; the other trained models drift less. We extend the test to four Aves Orders × 100 samples each (Passeriformes, Charadriiformes, Piciformes, Strigiformes); `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches cos 0.03–0.08 against the Class direction at L7 for all four orders. The directional claim — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches the random-orthogonality floor while random-init does not — is robust both within-manifest (bootstrap, B=30) and across stratified clip-swap resamples (5 seeds × 75% retention; mean 0.048 ± 0.020 for `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` vs 0.939 ± 0.027 for random-init, with no overlap; the across-resample spread is smaller than the within-manifest bootstrap CI for both). The *factored* reading requires a causal probing experiment we do not perform.

**Note on intermediate-cos models.** Across-resample spreads on §4.8 are tight at the two extremes (`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` near the floor: range 0.019–0.070; random-init near full alignment: range 0.89–0.96) but wide for the intermediate-cos trained models (`eat_all` mean 0.33, range 0.02–0.50; `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` mean 0.32, range 0.19–0.50). We read this as a property of the metric near intermediate values, not a property of the underlying geometry — the absolute |cos| of two centroid-difference directions can move substantially when small numbers of clips are swapped, but the directional ranking from random-init high-alignment to `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` low-alignment survives every resample. We do not report absolute values for the intermediate models as findings.

**§4.9 Trained models compress within-Class species detail relative to random-init.** Per-species centroids over the 12 species clearing a 5-sample threshold: random-init has the *highest* per-species separability ratio at every layer (0.33 at L12; bootstrap [0.31, 0.42]). `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` peaks at 0.20 [0.21, 0.22] at L10 and is the only trained model with substantial within-Class species structure. A reading consistent with §2's "random-init preserves input acoustic structure": random projections preserve acoustic distances between species, and trained models compress those acoustic differences in service of coarser invariances (bio-vs-non-bio at L9, Class at L7). The bio direction in §4 is acquired by sacrificing fine species detail, and random-init's higher species separability is compatible with — possibly evidence for — that compression-of-detail story.

## 6. Late-layer collapse splits the family — and the collapsing model's L12 dominant variance direction is the bio classifier

**§5 Frame-level L12 effective rank** splits the family by `_bio` vs not: `eat_all` 62.6, `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` 11.2 vs `eat_bio` 180.4, `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` 188.7. The non-bio fine-tune's L12 effective rank (11.2) is comparable to the random-init L12 effective rank (9.8) — but they get there by opposite routes (the trained model has compressed structure into a few directions; random-init has never *had* much structure to begin with, beyond what input acoustics provide), so the equality should not be read as "the SSL fine-tune undid the training."

**§5.1 The L12 collapse in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is concentration along one direction, not scalar shrinkage.** `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` L12 puts **61% of variance in one direction** vs 26% at L11. Both SSL fine-tunes scale up the L12 norm 3× over their pretrains; the bio variant amplifies many directions, the non-bio variant amplifies one.

**§5.2 The dominant L12 direction in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` aligns with the bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis.** Aligning the top-1 PC at L12 with the bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis gives `|cos| = 0.74 [0.67, 0.80]`, with Cohen's d = 0.86 along that direction for bio-vs-non-bio separation. No other trained model's L12 top-1 PC aligns with the bio axis (next-closest: `eat_bio` at 0.26 [0.06, 0.40]). The dominant variance direction of `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` at L12 is approximately the bio classifier; lower-eigenvalue directions still carry Order-discriminative information (see §4.11), so we do not claim the model has thrown out everything except bio — only that the *dominant* variance direction has aligned with the bio axis.

**§5.3 The §5.1 concentration is directional, not clip-collapse.** Within-clip frame variance over total variance stays ≥ 0.69 at every layer for trained models; the L12 concentration is purely directional and does not flatten frames within a single clip.

**§5.4 The dominant L12 direction in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is acquired specifically at L12.** Layer-wise, the alignment between top-1 PC and bio axis in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is near zero through L11 and jumps at L12. The directional concentration is a single-layer property, not a gradual sharpening across the SSL fine-tune.

## 7. Linear-probe corroboration of §4.7 (§4.11)

To address the concern that subspace cosines on centroid differences cannot, on their own, distinguish "the model has learned to encode Class as a feature" from "the model has learned acoustic invariances that incidentally separate Aves from Mammalia inputs," we add a non-geometric, non-centroid corroboration: linear-probe accuracy.

**Setup.** L2-regularized logistic regression on standardized frame activations, 80/20 stratified split at the *clip* level (so frames from a held-out clip are never seen during training), seed 42. Class probe = Aves (n=400 clips) vs Mammalia (n=200 clips), 30,000 frames, majority baseline 0.667. Order probe = Passeriformes (n=100 clips) vs other-Aves (Charadriiformes + Piciformes + Strigiformes, n=300 clips), within-Aves only, 20,000 frames, majority baseline 0.750.

**Reporting note.** The probe accuracies in the table below are from a v2 run with clip-level train/test splits. An earlier v1 run used a frame-level random split, which leaked frames from each clip into both train and test sets and inflated probe accuracies; the v1 numbers are deprecated and not reported here. The v2 results referenced in §5/§5.2 ("L12 retains 0.842 Order probe accuracy") will be updated once the v2 run completes. The directional ranking — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` highest at every layer, random-init at majority baseline on Order — is robust to the frame-vs-clip split choice in the v1 data and is the load-bearing claim of this section; the absolute numbers are conditioned on the clip-level v2 run.

| Model | mean Class probe acc (L5/L7/L9/L12) | mean Order probe acc (L5/L7/L9/L12) |
|---|---|---|
| `eat_all` | 0.920 | 0.835 |
| `eat_bio` | 0.911 | 0.825 |
| `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` | 0.925 | 0.842 |
| **`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`** | **0.957** | **0.900** |
| `random_init_eat_seed42` | 0.856 | 0.751 |

`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reads the highest Class **and** Order probe accuracy at every individual layer L5/L7/L9/L12, peaking at L9 with Class 0.981 and Order 0.944. The geometric ranking from §4.7 — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` > others > random-init — replicates in probe-accuracy space.

**Two follow-on observations.** (1) Random-init reads Class probe 0.85 (well above the 0.67 majority baseline) but Order probe 0.75 (*exactly* at majority baseline at every layer). Class is partly architectural — the random-init network produces a representation with ~85% linearly-recoverable Aves-vs-Mammalia structure from input acoustic statistics alone. Order is fully learned — random-init has zero linearly-recoverable Order structure. The trained-vs-random gap is therefore ~7–10% on Class and ~10–20% on Order; the "learned Order" effect is the more clean-cut. (2) `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` retains an Order probe accuracy of 0.842 at L12 — just as high as at L5/L7/L9 of the same model (0.83–0.85). Order-discriminative information survives the §5.1 mode collapse in low-eigenvalue directions that the centroid geometry of §5 cannot see. We use this as the basis for the §5.2 softening above.

**Methodological caveat.** We also run Iterative Nullspace Projection (Ravfogel et al. 2020) on the Class probe with `max_iters=15`, then test Order accuracy on the Class-nullspace activations. The survival ratio is *not* reported as a Class⊥Order test: with 15 1-D nullifications, Class probe accuracy drops only from 0.92 to 0.83, not to the chance floor. Class information is distributed across many directions in 768-d, and 15 1-D projections do not fully null it. Order survival reads 0.93–1.01 across all four trained models, which is partly a tautology of "Class wasn't really nulled." A more aggressive nullification (max_iters ≥ 80, or a multi-class probe so each iteration nulls a higher-dimensional subspace) would convert this into a defensible test; deferred.

## 8. Architecture sets the manifold; training expands the linear envelope

The MLE-ID(k=20) intrinsic dim reads 7–14 across (model, layer) for trained models — and 11–15 for random-init in the same range. Training does not widen the local manifold beyond what untrained EAT-base architecture provides. What training does widen is the **eff_rank / MLE-ID ratio**: ≈ 1 for random init, 17–43 for trained models. We document this as (n, k)-sensitive: MLE-ID grows monotonically with k and the absolute value depends on the estimator, but the eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio is robust across (n ∈ {3000, 10000, 30000}) × (k ∈ {5, 10, 20, 40, 80}) sweeps. The methodologically conservative claim is "training expands the linear envelope while keeping the local manifold dimension fixed at what architecture provides."

## 9. Discussion

The simplest reading: bio pretrain + SSL fine-tune on the union is the only ingredient combination in this 2×2 design that simultaneously produces a separated bio direction, the highest linearly-recoverable Class structure, within-Class species structure, and a Class/Order centroid-cosine at the random-orthogonality floor. The two ingredients in isolation produce fragments. Bio pretrain alone (`eat_bio`) produces a moderately-separated bio direction but no clean Class/Order near-orthogonality and middling species structure. SSL fine-tune alone (`sl_eat_all_ssl_all`) concentrates the L12 dominant variance direction onto the bio classifier, with retained-but-low-eigenvalue Order information. Only when both ingredients are present do all four geometric properties co-occur. Subject to the two confounds named in §1 (n=4 with no replicates, and SSL-as-new-data-domain-exposure for `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` only), this is a single observation, not an attribution.

**Two retractions from prior versions of this work** are worth recording: a layer-4 TwoNN dip we earlier reported as a learned property is a TwoNN(k=2) estimator artifact (MLE-ID(k=20) is stable at L4); and an earlier "L0 effective rank ≈ 3 across all four models" finding was a pooling artifact, with frame-level L0 eff_rank spreading 11–44.

**Limitations.** (i) n=4 trained models is the population, not a sample — claims are exact for this family, suggestive at best for transformers in general. The n=4 cannot disentangle ingredient effects from training-run specifics, and the SSL axis is confounded with new-data-domain exposure as detailed in §1. (ii) The 600-sample manifest is fixed by construction; bootstraps see sample-selection noise but not manifest-construction noise. A manifest-resampling sensitivity experiment (5 seeds × stratified clip-swap subsample of the existing 800-clip per-Order shards at 75% retention) confirms across-resample stability for the §4.7 L7 cos (spread ≤ 0.02 across all five models, well inside the within-manifest bootstrap CIs) and for the §4.8 L12 |cos| at the headline cell (`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` mean 0.048 ± 0.020, smaller than the bootstrap CI of [0.0036, 0.110]) and at the random-init anchor (mean 0.94 ± 0.03). The intermediate-cos trained models (`eat_all`, `sl_eat_all_ssl_all`) read wider across-resample spreads on §4.8 (range up to 0.50) — a property of the absolute-cos metric near intermediate values rather than of the underlying geometry, and consistent with §4.8 being reported as a directional ranking rather than an absolute number for those models. The clip-swap experiment does *not* address "what if the underlying 800 had been drawn from a different selection of NatureLM-audio-training files in the first place"; that would require re-extracting activations from a fresh draw and is not performed here. (iii) All evidence is geometric or linear-probe-based. We have not run activation patching, full INLP nullification (see §4.11 caveat), or any other independent test that would convert a directional claim ("the Aves and Mammalia centroids are far apart along a stable direction at L7, and a linear probe recovers that direction") into a representational claim ("the model encodes Class as a feature at L7, factored from Order"). Where we use language like "exhibits a direction" we mean the geometric and linear-probe statements only; readers seeking causal evidence should treat the findings as hypothesis-generating. (iv) No non-EAT control; the §3 frame-vs-pooled divergence is observed only in this family.

## Abstract

We probe four 13-layer audio transformers from the Earth Species Project's ESP-AVES2 EAT family — one bio-pretrained checkpoint, one general-audio checkpoint, and the SSL fine-tunes of each — against two frozen subsamples of NatureLM-audio-training: a 600-clip source-stratified manifest (100 × 7 source datasets, used for the bio/non-bio and pooling findings) and an 800-clip per-Order manifest (4 Aves Orders × 100 + 200 Mammalia + 200 non-bio, used for the Class / Order / species findings). Activations from all 13 layers of each model are anchored against a fifth control: the same EAT architecture with completely re-initialized weights. **The headline finding is universal across the four trained models: bidirectional iterative nullspace projection reveals an *asymmetric coupled hierarchy* between taxonomic Class (Aves vs Mammalia) and within-Class Order (Passeriformes vs other Aves) — nullifying Class destroys 0.057–0.218 of linear Order probe accuracy across the 11 cells where pre-nullification Order is at or above the 0.750 majority baseline, while symmetric-depth Order nullification leaves binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe accuracy within ±0.006 of pre-nullification across 16 trained-model cells.** A non-linear MLP probe at the headline `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` cells recovers a small subset of Order accuracy that the linear probe does not (post-null gap 0.015–0.083), so we frame the result as "the *linear* component of Order is encoded within the Class subspace." Random-init shows neither effect. The asymmetry — Class as the higher-level feature, the linear component of Order encoded as a refinement *within* the Class subspace rather than in a causally independent one — contrasts with the factored-subspace prediction of Park-Choe-Veitch (2024) for LLMs; we note that EAT lacks the unembedding-matrix structure grounding the PCV2024 prediction, so the non-transfer is structurally expected. Within the family, one checkpoint — the bio-pretrained model with SSL fine-tuning on the bio + non-bio union — concentrates several within-family geometric extrema: it (i) separates bio from non-bio at the subspace level mid-network, with a sharp threshold at 2.5% non-bio acoustic input shifting the L9 representation 44% of the full bio-to-non-bio range (§4.5 refined sweep); (ii) produces the strongest linearly-recoverable Class direction in the family (Aves vs Mammalia at layer 7, clip-level-split probe accuracy 0.90 vs 0.82 at random-init, and subspace cos 0.40); (iii) preserves the most within-Aves species structure of any trained model; and (iv) shows the largest Class-driven destruction of Order probe accuracy at L9 (linear probe 0.807 → 0.589, drop 0.218; MLP probe 0.851 → 0.672, drop 0.179), versus 0.066–0.123 linear-probe destruction at L7/L9 of the other three trained models. Beyond the headline, we report two methodological notes: frame-level and mean-pooled views of the geometry diverge heterogeneously across (model, layer); and the relationship between the linear envelope (effective rank) and the local manifold (MLE-ID) decouples during training, with the eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio rising from ≈1 in random-init to 17–43 in trained models while the manifold-dim estimate itself stays in 7–14. We make no claim about audio encoders generally; the population is a single architecture's four checkpoints plus its random-init control.

## 1. Introduction and scope

**What this paper is.** A within-family geometric audit of one published audio-encoder family. The pretraining-data axis (bio-only vs bio+non-bio) and the SSL-fine-tune axis (with/without) cross to give 4 cells; we add architecture-only random-init as a fifth. We hold the manifest, extraction protocol, and metric implementations fixed across all five, run frame-level analyses on 30,000 frames per (model, layer), and bootstrap each headline number.

**What this paper is not.** A claim about audio encoders in general, a claim about pooling in general, or a claim that we have found semantic features. We take centroids and subspaces seriously as descriptions of where mass sits in 768-dim space; we do not conflate "Aves and Mammalia centroids are far apart along a stable direction" with "the model has learned a bird concept."

**Why the EAT family.** Four checkpoints sharing architecture, training corpus structure, and tokenization, varying only in two binary training-axis choices, is rare. The minimal-pairs design lets us attribute geometric differences to ingredient combinations rather than to architectural confounds.

**Two unavoidable confounds the n=4 design cannot disentangle.** (a) With no replicates per cell, "the combination of bio-pretrain × SSL-fine-tune produces this geometry" is observationally indistinguishable from "the specific training run that produced `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` happened to land in a favorable initialization / data-order / learning-rate-schedule trajectory." (b) The SSL fine-tune is on the bio+non-bio union for both SSL variants, which means `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` is exposed to a *new* acoustic domain (non-bio) at fine-tune time on top of bio pretrain, while `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is not. The "SSL fine-tune" axis is therefore confounded with "exposure to a new acoustic domain at fine-tune time," and that second factor — not the SSL objective itself — is plausibly the active ingredient in any geometry difference between `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` and `eat_bio`. We name this confound here so readers can apply the appropriate skepticism throughout.

**The headline finding in one sentence.** Of the four trained checkpoints, only `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` (bio pretrain + SSL fine-tune) simultaneously exhibits a bio-vs-non-bio direction, the highest linearly-recoverable Class structure, within-Aves species structure, and a Class/Order centroid-cosine at the random-orthogonality floor at the output layer; the combination of ingredients matters relative to either ingredient alone, with the caveat above.

## 2. Setup and the random-init anchor

We use the standard EAT extraction (`model.pos_drop` + `model.blocks.0..11`, hidden dim 768, 513 patch tokens including a CLS-like index 0) on each of two frozen manifests. Frame-level subsampling draws 50 frames per clip from the valid-token range with seed 42; MLE-ID and TwoNN further subsample to 10,000 frames.

**Two manifests, used by different sections.** The findings in this paper come from two pre-registered subsamples of NatureLM-audio-training:

- **Source manifest** — `naturelm_by_source_100each_20260418T171459Z`, **600 clips = 100 × 7 source datasets** (Xeno-canto, iNaturalist, Animal Sound Archive, Watkins as bio; WavCaps, NatureLM-music, NSynth as non-bio). Used by §3 (frame-vs-pooled), §4 (bio-vs-non-bio direction), §4.5 (bio-axis mixing), §4.6 (per-source structure), §5 / §5.1 / §5.2 / §5.3 / §5.4 (late-layer collapse), §9 (eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio).
- **Per-Order manifest** — `naturelm_by_order_p100_m200_n200_20260427T222756Z`, **800 clips = 4 Aves Orders × 100 + 200 Mammalia + 200 non-bio**. Constructed because the source manifest has only 11 non-Passeriformes Aves clips, which is too few to test within-Aves Order structure at any reasonable statistical strength. Used by §4.7 (Aves vs Mammalia), §4.8 (Veitch hierarchy test), §4.9 (species barycenters), §4.11 (linear-probe corroboration), §4.12 (asymmetric INLP signature), and the manifest-resampling sensitivity in §10 / Limitations.

The two manifests share the same extraction pipeline and the same five models but cover different population structures; numbers from one cannot be directly compared to numbers from the other without restating which manifest was used. We name the source manifest by default in headline numbers and explicitly tag per-Order numbers as such throughout. The frame-level row count per (model, layer) is 30,000 on the source manifest (600 × 50) and 40,000 on the per-Order manifest (800 × 50).

The random-init baseline (`random_init_eat_seed42`) is the same EAT architecture instantiated and then completely reinitialized — `init_weights` plus per-module `reset_parameters`, with a `normal(0, 0.02)` fallback for the 2/150 parameters those paths miss. It receives the same audio through the same hooks. Init variability is checked at seeds 7 and 13 (eff_rank spread ≤ 1.3 across all layers vs trained-vs-random gaps of 200–350); we keep seed 42 as the anchor and treat the seed-spread as negligible relative to every claim's effect size.

**What random-init does and does not control for.** Random-init *passes audio through random transformer weights*; it does not output noise. The activations preserve substantial input-acoustic structure — random projections approximately preserve Euclidean distances, and patch-token attention with random weights still mixes acoustically-similar frames. We will see in §4.11 that random-init produces a Class probe accuracy of 0.85 (Aves vs Mammalia, majority baseline 0.67) — random-init "knows" Aves from Mammalia at 85% from acoustic input statistics alone, before any training. Random-init is therefore a "preserves input acoustic structure" control, not a "no structure" control. Where trained models differ from random-init we cannot in general distinguish "trained models added structure" from "trained models destroyed input structure"; both are happening. We flag specific cases in §4.9 (where random-init preserves *more* per-species detail than trained models, because trained models compress fine acoustic differences in service of coarser invariances) and in §5/§6 (where the comparison between random-init and `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` reads more meaningful than it is, because the two arrive at similar effective rank by opposite routes).

We use four geometric primitives: **effective rank** = `exp(-Σ p_i log p_i)` over normalized covariance eigenvalues; **participation ratio** = `(Σλ)² / Σλ²`; **intrinsic dimension** via TwoNN(k=2) and MLE-ID(k=20); **subspace overlap** = `mean(cos(principal_angles))` between top-10 PCA bases. All headlines are accompanied by B=30 to B=50 bootstrap CIs on the manifest.

## 3. Frame-level and mean-pooled views diverge heterogeneously across (model, layer)

Mean-pooled effective rank reads ≈ 3 at layer 0 of all four trained models. Frame-level effective rank at L0 reads 11/26/44/17 across the four. At later layers the divergence runs the other way: pooled TwoNN reads 9–11 across most (model, layer), while frame-level TwoNN drops to 3–5. The two views of the same activations diverge by a factor that depends on the (model, layer) cell.

We do not frame this as "pooling distorts geometry" — frame-level is not ground truth, and either view can be the right one depending on the downstream object of analysis. If a user of these embeddings will pool them for downstream classification, the *pooled* geometry is what matters for that user. What we do claim is that the divergence is heterogeneous: a finding about one view does not transfer cleanly to the other. The "L0 effective rank ≈ 3 across all four models" finding from an earlier version of this work is a case study — it survived because pooling collapsed heterogeneous L0 subspaces onto similar low-dim numbers, and the four L0 subspaces are visibly different at frame level. Every subsequent section uses frame-level activations because the geometric primitives in §2 (effective rank, MLE-ID, principal-angle subspace cosines) are defined on the unaggregated frame distribution; we do not claim frame-level geometry is privileged in general.

## 4. The bio direction lives in only one model and exhibits a non-linear input/representation map

**§4 Bio-vs-non-bio subspace cosine.** Top-10 subspace cosine between bio-pooled and non-bio-pooled centroids' PCA bases drops to **0.569 [0.556, 0.605]** at L9 of `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`. The other three trained models bottom out in [0.67, 0.81] in the same layer range; the random-init baseline reads 0.90 [0.89, 0.92]. The gap between `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` and the random-init anchor is 30× the within-manifest CI width.

**§4.5 The bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis exhibits a sharp threshold near α=0.** We sweep mix ratio α ∈ {0, 0.025, 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.0} on `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L9, with n_bio=5 × n_nonbio=5 = 25 mixture pairs per α. The bio-axis projection (mean across pairs) is sharply non-linear at low α: at **α=0.025 (just 2.5% non-bio audio), the projection has already shifted 44% of the full bio-to-non-bio range** (+0.40 → −0.37). At α=0.10 the shift is 53%; at α=0.25 it is 60%. Beyond α=0.025 the response is approximately linear in α (slope ≈ −0.95 per unit α over [0.025, 1.0]). The within-pair standard deviation of bio-axis projection also jumps at α=0.025 (0.40 → 0.82), suggesting the threshold is mixture-pair-specific rather than a uniform amplitude effect. The asymmetric input/representation map — a small acoustic perturbation producing a disproportionately large representational move — is *specific to* `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`; the other three trained models have near-zero or partial bio-axis range and the question of functional form is not even meaningful on them (cf. the cross-model table in the audio-mixing pilot). The wide bio-axis is unlocked by the *combination* of bio pretrain and SSL fine-tuning; within that wide axis, the input-to-representation map exhibits a threshold near α=0.025 followed by approximately linear response.

**§4.6 "Bio" is not a single coherent direction in any of the trained models.** Per-source pairwise frame-level distances reveal that within-bio source-pairs do not collapse onto a single point; Watkins (marine mammals) is geometrically more distant from Xeno-canto than several non-bio sources are from each other. The bio-vs-non-bio direction in §4 should be read as "the model separates the four bio source distributions from the three non-bio source distributions on average," not "the model has learned a bio-detector feature."

## 5. Class and within-Aves Order structure across the family

**§4.7 Aves vs Mammalia subspace cosine is lowest in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`.** Per-Class top-10 subspace cosine drops to **0.403 [0.386, 0.423]** at L7 of `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` — beating every other learned direction in this paper. The other trained models read 0.57–0.65 in the same layer range; random-init reads 0.91. The gap to random-init is 28× the within-manifest CI. We add a non-geometric corroboration of this finding in §4.11.

**§4.8 Centroid cosine for the Aves-Mammalia and Passeriformes-Aves directions: trained EAT models break the random-init alignment, but the headline cell does not reach the lower tail of an empirical permutation null.** Following Veitch et al.'s hierarchical-feature framing, we test |cos(Aves−Mammalia direction, Passer−Aves direction)| at every layer for all five models. Centroid cosines: random-init reads 0.92–0.94 across L7/L9/L12; the four trained models read substantially lower at L7/L9 (0.003–0.096 across cells), and split at L12 (`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` 0.069, `eat_bio` 0.100, `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` 0.307, `eat_all` 0.401). The directional ranking — random-init far above all trained models — is robust both within-manifest (bootstrap, B=30) and across stratified clip-swap resamples (5 seeds × 75% retention; `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L12 mean 0.048 ± 0.020 vs random-init L12 0.939 ± 0.027, no overlap). We extend the test to four Aves Orders × 100 samples each (Passeriformes, Charadriiformes, Piciformes, Strigiformes); `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches |cos| 0.03–0.08 against the Class direction at L7 for all four Orders, the only trained model with that property.

**Empirical permutation null.** To interpret the absolute magnitudes, we permute the Aves-Order labels of Aves clips and the Aves/Mammalia labels of Class clips B=200 times within the per-Order manifest, recomputing the two centroid-difference directions and their |cos| each time. The resulting null distribution is markedly higher than the theoretical √(2/(πd)) ≈ 0.029 floor of two independently-chosen unit vectors in 768-d: empirical null medians range 0.077 to 0.658 across (model, layer). Real centroid-difference vectors in this dataset, even on shuffled labels, retain alignment from input acoustic statistics that arbitrary unit vectors do not. The relevant comparison for the observed |cos| values is therefore the empirical null, not the theoretical floor. **What the empirical null revises.** The earlier reading — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches the 768-d random-orthogonality floor and therefore *factors* Class from Order — is not supported by these data. Three observations:

(1) Random-init lies in the *upper* tail of the empirical null at every tested layer (p_lower 0.875–0.950): its Aves-Mammalia and Passer-Aves centroid directions are *more aligned* than typical permutations of the same labels would produce. This is the quantity that is anomalous against the null. **The high empirical null medians (0.077–0.659) track the population's effective rank: across the 15 (model, layer) cells reported above, Pearson r(null_median, eff_rank) = −0.820 (p<0.001) and Spearman r = −0.907 (p<0.0001).** Per-Order-manifest effective rank ranges from 7.6 (random_init L12) to 348 (sl_eat_bio_ssl_all L7); null medians span 0.077 to 0.659. Two centroid-difference vectors estimated in a low-rank distribution are highly correlated by construction — the largest eigendirections dominate both estimates, even after label shuffling. Random-init's null medians sit at the high end because random-init has the lowest effective rank in the family (7.6–9.9). Trained models with higher effective rank produce lower null medians. The mechanism is geometric (low-rank → correlated random-direction estimates), not an "acoustic features survive label shuffling" story; only departures from the empirical null are interpretively meaningful.

**Note on multiple comparisons.** The 15 per-cell `p_lower` values reported above are descriptive: with 15 comparisons, no individual cell survives Bonferroni correction at α = 0.05/15 ≈ 0.0033, and the smallest observed p (eat_all L7, p_lower=0.015) does not survive Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control at q=0.05. The directional claim of this section — random-init reads ≫ trained models — is supported by the magnitude of the trained-vs-random gap (trained-model median |cos| at L7/L9 is 0.020 vs random-init 0.922, a **46× gap**) and does not require per-cell significance testing.

(2) Trained models lie in or near the typical-null region (p_lower 0.045–0.755). Two trained cells reach the conventional p < 0.05 lower-tail threshold: `eat_all` L7 (p_lower=0.015) and `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` L9 (p_lower=0.045). The headline cell — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L9, the layer at which §4.7's Class subspace cosine and §4.12's INLP signature both peak — reads p_lower=0.570, indistinguishable from the empirical null; L12 reads p_lower=0.380, also typical-null.

(3) The directional claim — trained models reduce centroid-cosine alignment relative to random-init — survives. The factored claim — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` cleanly factors Class from Order in its centroid directions — does not. The defensible reading is "trained EAT models break the acoustic-feature alignment that random-init exhibits between Class and within-Class Order centroid directions, but the headline model is not in the lower tail of the empirical null at the layers where its other geometric signatures peak."

For the deeper hierarchical-encoding question — does Order live in a subspace causally independent of Class? — the centroid cosine is the wrong instrument. It captures only the first moments of the population, not the linear subspaces that carry probe-detectable information. We address that question directly with bidirectional INLP in §4.12, which gives a different and more interpretable answer: Class is structurally above Order in the trained representations, but Order is encoded *within* the Class subspace rather than in a causally independent one.

**§4.9 Trained models compress within-Class species detail relative to random-init.** Per-species centroids over the 12 species clearing a 5-sample threshold: random-init has the *highest* per-species separability ratio at every layer (0.33 at L12; bootstrap [0.31, 0.42]). `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` peaks at 0.20 [0.21, 0.22] at L10 and is the only trained model with substantial within-Class species structure. A reading consistent with §2's "random-init preserves input acoustic structure": random projections preserve acoustic distances between species, and trained models compress those acoustic differences in service of coarser invariances (bio-vs-non-bio at L9, Class at L7). The bio direction in §4 is acquired by sacrificing fine species detail, and random-init's higher species separability is compatible with — possibly evidence for — that compression-of-detail story.

## 6. Late-layer collapse splits the family — and the collapsing model's L12 dominant variance direction is the bio classifier

**§5 Frame-level L12 effective rank** splits the family by `_bio` vs not: `eat_all` 62.6, `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` 11.2 vs `eat_bio` 180.4, `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` 188.7. The non-bio fine-tune's L12 effective rank (11.2) is comparable to the random-init L12 effective rank (9.8) — but they get there by opposite routes (the trained model has compressed structure into a few directions; random-init has never *had* much structure to begin with, beyond what input acoustics provide), so the equality should not be read as "the SSL fine-tune undid the training."

**§5.1 The L12 collapse in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is concentration along one direction, not scalar shrinkage.** `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` L12 puts **61% of variance in one direction** vs 26% at L11. Both SSL fine-tunes scale up the L12 norm 3× over their pretrains; the bio variant amplifies many directions, the non-bio variant amplifies one.

**§5.2 The dominant L12 direction in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` aligns with the bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis.** Aligning the top-1 PC at L12 with the bio-vs-non-bio centroid axis gives `|cos| = 0.74 [0.67, 0.80]`, with Cohen's d = 0.86 along that direction for bio-vs-non-bio separation. No other trained model's L12 top-1 PC aligns with the bio axis (next-closest: `eat_bio` at 0.26 [0.06, 0.40]). The dominant variance direction of `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` at L12 is approximately the bio classifier. We do not claim the model has destroyed all non-bio information at L12 — the §6 effective rank of 11.2 is small but non-zero, and the bio-vs-non-bio probe achieves Cohen's d = 0.86 (a strong but not perfect classifier) — only that the *dominant* variance direction has aligned with the bio axis. The clip-level v2 Order probe in §4.11 reads at majority baseline (0.749) at L12 of `sl_eat_all_ssl_all`, consistent with the §5.1 mode collapse having destroyed the Order-discriminative information that survived in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`'s wider L12 envelope.

**§5.3 The §5.1 concentration is directional, not clip-collapse.** Within-clip frame variance over total variance stays ≥ 0.69 at every layer for trained models; the L12 concentration is purely directional and does not flatten frames within a single clip.

**§5.4 The dominant L12 direction in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is acquired specifically at L12.** Layer-wise, the alignment between top-1 PC and bio axis in `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` is near zero through L11 and jumps at L12. The directional concentration is a single-layer property, not a gradual sharpening across the SSL fine-tune.

## 7. Linear-probe corroboration of §4.7 (§4.11)

To address the concern that subspace cosines on centroid differences cannot, on their own, distinguish "the model has learned to encode Class as a feature" from "the model has learned acoustic invariances that incidentally separate Aves from Mammalia inputs," we add a non-geometric, non-centroid corroboration: linear-probe accuracy.

**Setup.** L2-regularized logistic regression on standardized frame activations, 80/20 stratified split at the *clip* level (so frames from a held-out clip are never seen during training), seed 42. Class probe = Aves (n=400 clips) vs Mammalia (n=200 clips), 30,000 frames, majority baseline 0.667. Order probe = Passeriformes (n=100 clips) vs other-Aves (Charadriiformes + Piciformes + Strigiformes, n=300 clips), within-Aves only, 20,000 frames, majority baseline 0.750.

**Reporting note.** Probe accuracies are from the v2 run with clip-level train/test splits (frames from a held-out clip never appear in training). An earlier v1 run used a frame-level random split that leaked clip-specific spectral features into the training set and inflated probe accuracies by ~3–13% across cells; v1 numbers are deprecated. The clip-level v2 numbers below are roughly 0.04 lower on Class and 0.05–0.13 lower on Order compared to v1.

Mean probe accuracy across L5/L7/L9/L12 per model (B=1 fixed split, seed 42):

| Model | mean Class probe acc (base 0.667) | mean Order probe acc (base 0.750) |
|---|---|---|
| `eat_all`              | 0.873 | 0.757 |
| `eat_bio`              | 0.858 | 0.759 |
| `sl_eat_all_ssl_all`   | 0.886 | 0.764 |
| **`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`** | **0.898** | **0.787** |
| `random_init_eat_seed42` | 0.819 | 0.687 |

`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reads the highest Class probe accuracy at every individual layer L5/L7/L9/L12 and the highest Order probe accuracy at L7/L9/L12 (tied with `eat_all` at L5), peaking at L9 with Class 0.912 and Order 0.807. The geometric ranking from §4.7 — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` > others > random-init — replicates in probe-accuracy space, but the magnitudes are modest. The Class spread among trained models is 0.05 (0.86–0.90); the Order spread among trained models is just 0.03 (0.76–0.79). We do not claim `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` is uniquely the only model with Order discrimination — it is the consistently-highest model, but the gap to the next-best trained model is small.

**Three follow-on observations.** (1) Random-init reads Class probe 0.82 (well above the 0.67 majority baseline) but Order probe 0.69 (*below* the 0.75 majority baseline). Class is partly architectural — the random-init network produces a representation with ~82% linearly-recoverable Aves-vs-Mammalia structure from input acoustic statistics alone, ~6 percentage points below the best trained model. Order is fully learned — random projections don't even reach majority baseline on Order, so any trained accuracy above 0.75 is learned. The trained-vs-random gap is ~6–10% on Class and ~7–10% on Order; in absolute magnitude the two are similar, but the Order signal is interpretively cleaner because random-init starts below baseline. (2) The largest trained-vs-random gap on Order is at `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L9 (0.807) — 12% above random-init L9 (0.685) and 5.7% above the majority baseline. This is a small effect by ML-literature standards but a real one. (3) `sl_eat_all_ssl_all` retains an Order probe accuracy of 0.749 at L12 — at the majority baseline, indistinguishable from chance. Order-discriminative information does *not* survive the §5.1 mode collapse for that model; the v1 reading "L12 retains 0.842 Order accuracy" was a frame-leakage artifact. The §5.2 softening above is unaffected — the dominant variance direction is the bio classifier — but we no longer claim Order information is preserved at L12 of `sl_eat_all_ssl_all`.

**Methodological caveat.** We also run Iterative Nullspace Projection (Ravfogel et al. 2020) on the Class probe with `max_iters=15`, then test Order accuracy on the Class-nullspace activations. The survival ratio is *not* reported as a Class⊥Order test: with 15 1-D nullifications, Class probe accuracy drops only from 0.92 to 0.83, not to the chance floor. Class information is distributed across many directions in 768-d, and 15 1-D projections do not fully null it. Order survival reads 0.93–1.01 across all four trained models, which is partly a tautology of "Class wasn't really nulled." A more aggressive nullification (max_iters ≥ 80, or a multi-class probe so each iteration nulls a higher-dimensional subspace) would convert this into a defensible test; deferred.

## 8. Asymmetric INLP signature: Class survives Order-nullification, Order does not survive Class-nullification (§4.12)

**Motivation.** §4.8 reports a centroid-cosine signature — the Aves-vs-Mammalia direction and the Passeriformes-vs-other-Aves direction approach the random-orthogonality floor in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` at L12 — which on its own is consistent with two distinct geometric pictures: (a) a Veitch-style factored hierarchy in which Class and Order live in causally independent linear subspaces, or (b) a hierarchy in which Order is encoded *within* the Class subspace as a refinement, with the centroid-difference vectors happening to be near-orthogonal in their first moments. To distinguish these we probe the relationship causally: nullify one feature and ask whether the other survives.

**Setup.** We run four complementary INLP-style nullification tests (Ravfogel et al. 2020) on layers L5/L7/L9/L12 of all five models, on the per-Order manifest (n=800 clips). All probes are L2-regularized logistic regression with a clip-level 80/20 train/test split (seed 42) — frames from a held-out clip never appear in training — and projection directions are computed from training-frame coefficients only. Probe accuracy is reported on the held-out clips' frames.

- **Class-first (step8).** A 5-way multi-class Class probe (4 Aves Orders × 100 clips + Mammalia × 200) is taken to chance with `max_iters=80, acc_floor=0.30`. Each iteration nulls a 4-D subspace (one direction per class), so cumulative nullification reaches roughly 320 dimensions. Once the 5-class probe is at chance (≈ 0.20 for 5-way; observed 0.31–0.39 in cells that ran the full 80 iterations), we re-train (i) a binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe and (ii) a binary Order probe (Passer vs other-Aves, within Aves only) on the Class-nulled activations.
- **Order-first (step9).** The binary Order probe is taken toward floor with `max_iters=40, acc_floor=0.55`. This nullification is *partial*: Order accuracy drops 0.02–0.07 in most cells. We then re-train a binary Class probe on the Order-nulled activations.
- **Multi-class Order INLP (step14).** Symmetric counterpart to step8: a 4-class Order probe (Passer vs 3 other Aves Orders) is taken to chance with `max_iters=80, acc_floor=0.30`. Each iteration nulls 3-D, cumulative ~21–54 dimensions in cells that broke early; ~80 in cells that did not. We then re-train a binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe.
- **MLP Order probe (step11 Round B).** At `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L5/L7/L9/L12, we additionally train a one-hidden-layer MLP probe (256 ReLU units, L2-regularized, early stopping) for Order classification on Class-nulled activations, alongside the linear probe. Tests whether non-linear Order signal survives Class nullification undetected by the linear probe.

We therefore frame the asymmetric coupling as **the *linear* component of Order is encoded within the Class subspace**: linear projection onto the nullspace of the 5-class Class probe destroys 0.06–0.22 of linear Order accuracy in the 11 retained cells, while a non-linear probe recovers a subset of the Order signal that survives the linear projection. The strict claim "Order is encoded within the Class subspace" would require the MLP probe to also drop to majority; it does not, so we report the linear-component framing.

**The picture.** Class is structurally above Order in the trained representations, in two distinct senses:

1. *Linear:* Order is encoded in a subspace that linearly contains the Class direction(s), so removing Class destroys 0.06–0.22 of linear Order discrimination.
2. *Causal/symmetric:* This holds even at INLP depths matched between the two directions (Result 4) — Order nullification at ~50 cumulative-D does not perturb Class.

Class, conversely, is encoded in a subspace that does not depend on Order at the 5-class-probe level (Result 3) and at the symmetric-depth multi-class Order level (Result 4) — it survives unscathed when Order is removed. We read this as an **asymmetric coupled hierarchy**: Class is the higher-level feature; the linear component of Order is encoded within the Class subspace as a refinement, rather than the **factored hierarchy** (independent subspaces) predicted by Park-Choe-Veitch (2024) for LLMs.

The §4.8 small centroid cosine and the §4.12 asymmetric INLP signature capture *independent* geometric properties:

- The §4.8 centroid cosine is a property of the *first moments* — where the Aves-Mammalia and Passer-Aves population means sit relative to each other.
- The §4.12 INLP signature is a property of the *full linear subspaces* that carry probe-detectable information.

A model can have nearly-orthogonal centroid-difference axes while still encoding both features in overlapping linear subspaces. `eat_all` L7 illustrates the dissociation: the §4.8 |cos| at that cell is at the lower end of the empirical permutation null (p_lower=0.015 before correction), yet step8 destroys 0.11 of its Order accuracy under Class nullification (clearly coupled by INLP). Both readings are correct; neither implies the other; both belong on the table. We therefore recommend reporting both metrics in any subsequent Veitch-style hierarchical-feature audit, and explicitly when they diverge.

**Robustness caveats.**

(1) *Passer ⊂ Aves containment.* Passeriformes is a strict subset of Aves, so the Aves-vs-Mammalia direction is a direction along which the Passer-clip centroid is itself displaced — Passer clips are Aves clips. Nulling Aves-vs-Mammalia therefore mechanically reduces the magnitude of any vector along which Passer differs from the other Aves Orders to the extent that those Orders are *also* displaced along the Aves-vs-Mammalia axis. The 0.06–0.22 Order destruction in Result 1 is therefore a true causal signature in our setting, but its absolute magnitude would partially survive even in a counterfactual representation where Order were geometrically independent of Class, because the population to which the Order probe applies (Aves-only) is itself defined by the feature being nulled. A pure test would null a Class direction defined by classes neither of which contains Passeriformes (e.g., Mammalia-vs-Insecta) and re-test Order on Aves; the per-Order manifest's class counts (Insecta = 2, Amphibia = 6) do not support this. We flag the structural containment as a partial confound on the absolute magnitude of the Order destruction. The Class⇄Order asymmetry — that Order does not destroy Class even at matched-depth nullification (Result 4), while Class destroys Order substantially — does not have a comparable structural concern: the Mammalia, Insecta, and Amphibia clips that contribute to the Class signal are not contained within any Order class, so nulling Order cannot mechanically displace their centroids.

(2) *INLP iteration sweep.* We checkpoint the cumulative projection P at iter counts {10, 20, 40, 80} cumulative-D nulled, and report Order linear probe accuracy at each (step11). Two patterns emerge. For the headline model `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`, Order accuracy decreases monotonically as Class nullification depth increases (e.g., L9: 0.738 at n=10 → 0.674 at n=20 → 0.608 at n=40 → 0.589 at n=80). For the other three trained models, Order destruction is non-monotonic with peak destruction at n=20, then partial recovery at n=80 — INLP first nulls broad directions carrying mixed Class+Order info, then becomes more "surgical" toward Class-only directions, partially restoring Order. In both patterns, **Order destruction at low iteration count (n=10, ~40 cumulative-D nulled) is non-zero (0.05–0.16 across non-degenerate cells)**, and substantially larger than the Order-on-Class destruction (≤ 0.001) reported in Result 3. The asymmetry survives at iteration depths matched between Class-first and Order-first INLP, complementing the symmetric multi-class Order test of Result 4.

(3) *Class nullification is defined relative to the 5-class probe.* Result 2 (binary Aves-vs-Mammalia re-test) addresses this directly. In 14 of 16 cells, the binary Class probe re-trained on Class-nulled activations reads at the majority baseline; the directions discoverable by a 5-class probe are the same directions a binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe needs. In the 2 cells where 5-class INLP broke early at `acc_floor=0.30` (eat_bio L5, sl_eat_all_ssl_all L12), residual binary Class signal remains; we flag those cells and do not include them in the headline range.

**Relationship to Park-Choe-Veitch 2024.** The factored-hierarchy prediction from PCV2024 is that hierarchically-related concepts in LLM residual streams live in linear subspaces that are causally independent under linear nullification — nulling Class should not affect Order, and vice versa. Across all four trained EAT-family encoders we observe the opposite: at matched INLP depth (Result 4), Class nullification destroys 6–22% of Order probe accuracy, while Order nullification leaves Class probe accuracy unchanged. We note that EAT lacks the unembedding-matrix structure PCV2024's factored prediction is grounded in (audio encoders have no token vocabulary), so the non-transfer is structurally expected; this finding should be read as an empirical corroboration of that structural prediction rather than as a refutation of PCV2024 within its native LLM setting. Whether the asymmetric coupling we observe is a property of audio encoders specifically, of small models specifically, of supervised+SSL training specifically, or of small encoder + large-output-vocabulary mismatch is a question the n=4 design cannot settle. What it does settle, modulo the caveats above, is that the LLM-derived factored prediction does not transfer to this family — and that the asymmetric coupled hierarchy is a defensible alternative reading consistent with both the centroid-cosine evidence in §4.8 and the probe-causal evidence here.

## 9. Architecture sets the manifold; training expands the linear envelope

The MLE-ID(k=20) intrinsic dim reads 7–14 across (model, layer) for trained models — and 11–15 for random-init in the same range. Training does not widen the local manifold beyond what untrained EAT-base architecture provides. What training does widen is the **eff_rank / MLE-ID ratio**: ≈ 1 for random init, 17–43 for trained models. We document this as (n, k)-sensitive: MLE-ID grows monotonically with k and the absolute value depends on the estimator, but the eff_rank/MLE-ID ratio is robust across (n ∈ {3000, 10000, 30000}) × (k ∈ {5, 10, 20, 40, 80}) sweeps. The methodologically conservative claim is "training expands the linear envelope while keeping the local manifold dimension fixed at what architecture provides."

## 10. Discussion

**Headline.** Across the 4-model EAT family, **the *linear* component of Order is encoded within the Class subspace** at every tested layer of every trained model, in a structurally asymmetric pattern: Class destruction propagates to Order, while Order destruction does not propagate to Class even at matched INLP nullification depth. Specifically, in the 11 cells where pre-nullification Order accuracy is at or above the majority baseline, a Class probe taken to chance pulls Order linear-probe accuracy down by 0.06–0.22 (largest at `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` L9, drop 0.218). Symmetric multi-class Order INLP at matched cumulative-D nulled (Result 4 of §4.12) leaves the binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe within ±0.006 of pre-nullification accuracy across 16 trained-model cells. A binary Aves-vs-Mammalia probe re-trained on Class-nulled activations reads at majority baseline in 14 of 16 cells, confirming the 5-class INLP nulls binary Class signal as well. A non-linear MLP probe recovers some Order signal that survives Class nullification (post-null gap +0.015 to +0.083 across `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` headline cells), so we frame the result as a *linear-component* claim. We name this picture **asymmetric coupled hierarchy**: Class as the higher-level feature, the linear component of Order encoded within the Class subspace as a refinement, with non-linear residual Order signal partially surviving the linear projection. Random-init shows neither effect — Class nullification *increases* Order accuracy by ~0.05 across every layer at seed 42 (L5 +0.045, L7 +0.049, L9 +0.049, L12 +0.052). Cross-seed cheap-mitigation reads at L9 confirm the sign is robust to seed choice: seed 7 reads +0.048, seed 13 reads +0.061; across three random-init seeds at L9 the mean is +0.053 (range +0.048 to +0.061).

**Contrast with the LLM Veitch picture.** Park-Choe-Veitch (2024) argue that hierarchically-related concepts in LLM residual streams live in linear subspaces that are causally independent under linear nullification — nullifying Class should not affect Order, and nullifying Order should not affect Class. Across all four trained EAT-family encoders we observe the opposite asymmetric pattern. We note that EAT lacks the unembedding-matrix structure that grounds the PCV2024 prediction (audio encoders have no token vocabulary), so this finding should be read as an empirical corroboration of the structural prediction (factored hierarchy is a property of LLM residual streams, not of small audio encoders) rather than as a refutation of PCV2024 within its native LLM setting. We do not claim our pattern generalizes beyond audio encoders, beyond small models, or beyond the supervised+SSL training regime sampled here. The §4.12 caveats (Passer⊂Aves structural containment; non-linear MLP residual) bound the strength of the alternative claim, but the qualitative direction — Class destroys Order, Order does not destroy Class even at matched depth — is uniform across all 4 trained models, all 4 tested layers, and survives the random-init anchor as a sanity check.

**Centroid cosines and INLP nullification capture different geometric properties.** §4.8 and §4.12 are independent measurements with independent answers, and the dissociation is methodologically load-bearing. `eat_all` L7 reads in the bottom 1.5% of the §4.8 empirical permutation null (looks "factored" by centroid cosine) yet has 0.11 of its Order accuracy destroyed under §4.12 Class nullification (looks "coupled" by INLP). A model can have nearly-orthogonal centroid-difference axes while still encoding both features in overlapping linear subspaces. Centroid cosine is a property of *first moments*; INLP nullification is a property of *full linear subspaces that carry probe-detectable information*. Single-metric replications of Veitch-style hierarchical-feature tests — i.e., centroid-cosine tests without an accompanying nullification test — risk reporting either evidence as both. We recommend reporting both, and explicitly when they diverge.

**Ingredient combinations in the 2×2 design.** Within the family, the bio-pretrain × SSL-fine-tune 2×2 produces a single configuration in which all five trained-model geometric properties co-occur: a separated bio direction (§4), the highest Class subspace cosine (§4.7), the highest linearly-recoverable Class and Order probe accuracy (§4.11), substantive within-Class species structure (§4.9), and the largest Class-driven Order destruction (§4.12) — `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`. The two ingredients in isolation produce fragments. Bio pretrain alone (`eat_bio`) produces a moderately-separated bio direction but smaller §4.7 Class subspace cosine, smaller §4.12 Order destruction, and middling species structure. SSL fine-tune alone (`sl_eat_all_ssl_all`) concentrates the L12 dominant variance direction onto the bio classifier (§5.2), and at L12 its Order probe accuracy drops to majority baseline (§4.11) — Order is destroyed by the §5.1 mode collapse rather than retained-and-coupled-to-Class as in `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all`. Only when both ingredients are present do all five geometric properties co-occur. Subject to the two confounds named in §1 (n=4 with no replicates, and SSL-fine-tune-introduces-new-data-domain-exposure for `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` only), this is a single observation, not an attribution.

**Two retractions from prior versions of this work** are worth recording. A layer-4 TwoNN dip we earlier reported as a learned property is a TwoNN(k=2) estimator artifact (MLE-ID(k=20) is stable at L4). An earlier "L0 effective rank ≈ 3 across all four models" finding was a pooling artifact, with frame-level L0 eff_rank spreading 11–44. A third reframe is large enough to flag separately: the v0 reading of §4.8 — "`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` reaches the 768-d random-orthogonality floor and therefore factors Class from Order" — is *demoted* in this version to "trained models break random-init's centroid-direction alignment, but the headline cell does not reach the lower tail of an empirical permutation null." The factored-hierarchy reading of v0 is replaced by §4.12's asymmetric coupled hierarchy as the load-bearing characterization.

**Limitations.** (i) n=4 trained models is the population, not a sample — claims are exact for this family, suggestive at best for transformers in general. The n=4 cannot disentangle ingredient effects from training-run specifics, and the SSL axis is confounded with new-data-domain exposure as detailed in §1. (ii) The 600-sample manifest is fixed by construction; bootstraps see sample-selection noise but not manifest-construction noise. A manifest-resampling sensitivity experiment (5 seeds × stratified clip-swap subsample of the existing 800-clip per-Order shards at 75% retention) confirms across-resample stability for the §4.7 L7 cos (spread ≤ 0.02 across all five models, well inside the within-manifest bootstrap CIs) and for the §4.8 L12 |cos| at the headline cell (`sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` mean 0.048 ± 0.020) and at the random-init anchor (mean 0.94 ± 0.03). The intermediate-cos trained models (`eat_all`, `sl_eat_all_ssl_all`) read wider across-resample spreads on §4.8 (range up to 0.50) — a property of the absolute-cos metric near intermediate values rather than of the underlying geometry. The clip-swap experiment does *not* address "what if the underlying 800 had been drawn from a different selection of NatureLM-audio-training files in the first place"; that would require re-extracting activations from a fresh draw and is not performed here. (iii) Evidence is geometric, linear-probe, or INLP-nullification-based. The §4.12 INLP test gives causal evidence at the *probe* level — a Class probe taken to chance does measurably destroy linear Order probe accuracy, while a non-linear MLP probe recovers a subset of the surviving Order signal (post-null linear-vs-MLP gaps 0.015–0.083 at `sl_eat_bio_ssl_all` headline cells). We do not run activation patching, behavioral interventions on a downstream task, or any test that converts a representational claim into a behavioral one ("the model uses Class as a precondition for Order").

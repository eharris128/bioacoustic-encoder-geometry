# Where to send the ESP-AVES2 geometric audit

**TMLR is the right primary target, with ICLR 2027 and ICASSP 2027's 8+1-page OJSP track as the strongest archival conference backups.** The paper's combination — empirical-only, geometric-only (no probes, no causal interventions), n=4 trained checkpoints, single bioacoustic encoder family, ~8 pages — is a poor structural match for the *main tracks* of NeurIPS/ICML/Interspeech, where reviewers reliably ask "where's the linear probe?" and "why should we care about one model family?". It is, however, an excellent match for venues whose acceptance criteria explicitly do not require novelty/SOTA (TMLR), whose subject area lists "visualization and interpretation of learned representations" alongside audio and biology (ICLR), whose page format is exactly 8+1 (ICASSP-OJSP), or whose central premise is comparing representations across models (NeurReps, UniReps, TAG-ML — all PMLR-archival workshop tracks). The strict archival preference, a non-trivial constraint, eliminates several otherwise-attractive workshops (SciForDL, Re-Align, Climate Change AI, AI-for-Animal-Communication) from the top of the ranking even though their topical fit is high.

The other structural constraint is timing. **Today is 2026-04-28**, which means ICML 2026 main (Jan), ICLR 2026 BlogPost (Dec 2025), Interspeech 2026 (Feb), and the ICLR 2026 GRaM/Re-Align workshops are all **already closed**. ICML 2026 workshops including the Mechanistic Interpretability workshop (May 8 deadline) are within ~10 days but are non-archival and a high-risk fit for geometry-only evidence. The realistic archival-with-runway targets cluster around **May 2026 (NeurIPS main + E&D), September 2026 (ICASSP 2027 + ICLR 2027), rolling (TMLR + JASA + TASLP), and Aug–Sep 2026 (NeurIPS workshops once announced)**.

## Top 3, ranked, with justifications

### 1. TMLR (primary recommendation)

TMLR is the cleanest archival fit on every axis the paper actually has. Its review criteria are explicitly **"are the claims supported by accurate, convincing and clear evidence" and "would some segment of the TMLR audience be interested"** — *not* novelty, *not* SOTA, *not* methodological proposal. This is a meaningfully different bar than NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR and was designed for exactly the class of work the paper represents: rigorous empirical analysis of an existing model family, with negative or scoped findings, in a niche domain. The journal has no hard page limit but optimizes review speed for ≤12 pages of main content, so ~8 pages slots in comfortably. Submissions are double-blind on OpenReview, satisfying the anonymization preference. Critically, since January 2025 TMLR papers are eligible for the **Journal-to-Conference track at NeurIPS, ICLR, and ICML**, meaning a TMLR acceptance can convert to a conference presentation without re-review — this preserves the conference-presence goal that an author choosing between TMLR and ICLR is usually weighing. Bioacoustics is a neutral domain at TMLR, neither strength nor weakness; the geometric-only methodology will *not* trigger a "where's the probe?" desk reject.

**Concrete revision required:** Expand the limitations section significantly and incorporate the in-progress INLP follow-up before submission. TMLR rewards thoroughness over novelty, so reviewers will probe the n=4 power, the 600-sample manifest construction, and the §4.8 Veitch-test concern about random-orthogonality floor at 768-d. Soften §4.8 from "factored hierarchy" to "drift toward a random-orthogonality floor that random-init does not exhibit" *in the paper itself*, exactly as the brief flags, and let the INLP result serve as one piece of representational evidence beyond pure geometry.

### 2. ICLR 2027 main conference

ICLR is the highest-fit *top-tier conference* for this paper, and the deadline (~September 23–25, 2026) gives ~5 months of polish runway from today. The CFP explicitly enumerates **"visualization or interpretation of learned representations," "audio," and "applications to physical sciences (physics, chemistry, biology, etc.)"** as in-scope subject areas — the paper sits at the intersection of all three. Crucially, ICLR has the strongest *track record* among top venues of accepting geometry-only papers without behavioral validation: Pope et al. 2021 (intrinsic dimension of images), Ansuini et al. 2019 (TwoNN intrinsic dim across DNN layers), and Park, Choe & Veitch 2024 (linear representation hypothesis and hierarchy in LLMs) are all archetypal precedents whose methodology the submitted paper directly inherits. The 9-page main + unlimited appendix format absorbs an 8-page body comfortably, and the OpenReview public-discussion phase, while it will surface "where's the probe?" questions, also gives the authors space to defend the geometric-only choice in real time. Bioacoustics is neutral here, not a strength — the framing must lead with general representation-geometry questions and use ESP-AVES2 as the empirical object, not the other way around.

**Concrete revision required:** Reframe the abstract and introduction so the central question is methodological-general ("what does a within-family geometric audit reveal about how training combinations shape hierarchical structure?") with bioacoustics as the case study, *not* the headline. Reviewers will interpret the paper through the lens of Park-Choe-Veitch and Ansuini, so foreground the methodological by-products (mean-pooling distortion of patch-token transformer geometry; decoupling of effective rank vs. intrinsic dimension during training, ratio rising 1→17–43 while MLE-ID stays at 7–14) as findings of independent ML-community interest.

### 3. ICASSP 2027 — IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing (OJSP) track

Among top audio venues, ICASSP 2027's OJSP track is the only one whose **page format is exactly 8 + 1** — a perfect structural match — and it produces a gold-OA archival journal paper (in IEEE OJSP) plus an in-person ICASSP presentation. The expected deadline is ~September 8–10, 2026, parallel to ICLR 2027. The Audio and Acoustic Signal Processing TC is the most representation-friendly TC at ICASSP and has accepted interpretability-only papers in 2026 (e.g., neural-audio-codec interpretability work, audio SAE analyses). Bioacoustics is neutral-to-strength: ICASSP has steady bioacoustics presence and occasional dedicated sessions. The principal risk is the "no downstream task" choice — ICASSP reviewers, more than ICLR's, expect at least one classification or clustering result to anchor geometric claims. Mitigation is straightforward and low-cost without compromising the paper's thesis (see below).

**Concrete revision required:** Add a single minimal kNN species-classification probe at the four implicated layers (L7 Aves/Mammalia, L9 bio/non-bio, L10 within-Aves species, L12 Class⊥Order) using the existing 600-sample manifest, purely as a *correlational sanity check* on whether the geometric structure is decodable. Frame it explicitly as "we add this not as evidence for the geometric claims but to satisfy the conventional audio-encoder reader's prior." This converts the most common reviewer objection into a paragraph in §5.

## Venues to flag explicitly

**Likely desk-reject or strong methods critique on geometric-only grounds:**
- **NeurIPS 2026 main track** — risk is high; "where's the probe / where's the causal validation?" is the single most common reviewer complaint for geometry-only submissions, and the niche-domain × small-n combination compounds it. The new "Concept & Feasibility" or "Negative Results" contribution types help but do not eliminate the risk.
- **ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability workshop** (deadline May 8, ~10 days out) — mechinterp orthodoxy heavily weights causal patching/SAEs/circuits; geometry-only is regularly read as "pre-mechinterp." Geometry of Categorical and Hierarchical Concepts (ICML 2024 1st prize) is a precedent but the exception, not the rule.
- **Interspeech 2027** — speech-only scope makes bioacoustics a real weakness, *and* reviewers reliably expect a phone/speaker/language ID probe.

**Domain mismatch (scope, not methodology, is the problem):**
- **EMNLP 2026 / ACL 2026 / BlackboxNLP 2026** — NLP-only by definition; bioacoustic encoders read as out-of-scope absent a strong language-analogous framing.
- **Methods in Ecology and Evolution / Ecological Applications / Remote Sensing of Environment** — top archival ecology journals but require explicit ecological deliverables (occupancy, population inference, conservation decision) the paper has explicitly excluded.
- **Climate Change AI workshops** — require a climate-impact pathway; encoder geometry has only an indirect biodiversity-monitoring connection and 4-page limit conflicts.

**Bioacoustics as a strength vs. neutral vs. weakness across the shortlist:**
- **Strength:** DCASE/BioDCASE 2026, AI for Non-Human Animal Communication (NeurIPS 2025 instance, possibly recurring), JASA, Ecological Informatics, IBAC 2027 (but the latter is an abstract congress, not a paper venue and audience is biologists).
- **Neutral:** TMLR, ICLR 2027, ICASSP 2027, TASLP, NeurIPS E&D, NeurReps, UniReps, TAG-ML, NLDL — domain doesn't help or hurt.
- **Weakness:** Interspeech (speech-only), EMNLP/ACL/BlackboxNLP (NLP-only), MechInterp workshop (LLM-overwhelmingly), Climate Change AI (impact-pathway requirement).

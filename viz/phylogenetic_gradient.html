Probe accuracy gradients track phylogenetic distance

Linear (LORO cross-validated) probes read out species identity from each of 13 layers of the ESP bioacoustic transformer. Closely related species need deeper layers and never fully separate; distantly related ones split apart in the first transformer block. Hover any line or point to trace one species pair across both views.

Peak accuracy vs phylogenetic distance

Probes: logistic regression on PCA-reduced layer activations, leave-one-recording-out cross-validation. Phylogenetic distances (millions of years since divergence) from TimeTree + literature. Data: results/phylogenetic_gradient_data.csv · ESP-AVES2 EAT encoder.
